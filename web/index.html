        What the numbers say

        The Video Games challenger improved NDCG@10 from 0.00715 to 0.01017 in a corrected exploratory replay. In the untouched Musical Instruments category it rose from 0.00801 to 0.00868. Absolute scores remain low, and new catalog items account for many misses. The conditional interval on Musical Instruments barely excludes zero.

        What the demo proves

        This loopback service supports a real locally trained bundle, reports route and fallback reasons, exposes health and aggregate request metrics, and never writes submitted histories to a log. The in-browser profiles are synthetic. Local ranking time is not a production latency claim.
